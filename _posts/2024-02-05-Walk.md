<div class="poem">
Excited to begin our long stravaig
His tail is up. Unleashed, he leaps away
On scent-laced trail through heather round the craig
He pauses, sniffs, and dawdles down the brae

Then spies the distant sea. He’s gone. No sign.
No whistle brings him back. Yet he knows well
I’ll find him in the rattling beach’s brine
And chuck wee stones for chasing through the swell.

Reluctant to accept the homeward trudge
Wet through and cold, he follows as I climb.
I towel him down and think myself his drudge
Yet loving brush grooms out the salty grime.

Exhausted, he’s asleep. I watch him twitch
And dream of walks, which both our lives enrich.
</div>
