<div class="poem">
Winter beech leaf
Peaty burn
Soaring buzzard
Withered fern

Fallen pinecone
Fir tree bark
Muddy puddle
Morning lark

Glistening seaweed
Heathery beinn
Wooden fencepost
Furtive wren

Frisky otter
Startled deer
Dusty sparrow
Pint of beer

Glass of whisky
Had a skid
Crashed the motor
Coffin lid.
</div>

Published in GALLUS, "Poetry Scotland's sassy sibling", issue 10, autumn 2025.
