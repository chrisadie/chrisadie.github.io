This is a [*cento*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cento_(poetry)) or "patchwork poem", which takes and re-purposes lines from other people to create something new. It involved two stages.

First, from a website of Pink Floyd lyrics I selected some lines which appealed to me, without thinking too much about their context or how I might use them.

Then, from those lines, I built a kind of narrative, using perhaps half of the lines I'd collected.
I hope you like it.
