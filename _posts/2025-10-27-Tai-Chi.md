I started training in Tai Chi Chuan in late 1992 or early 1993. After a couple of years under
David Welsh, I moved to the class at Edinburgh's Meadowbank Stadium, run by Ian Cameron, David's teacher.
Ian's school of [Five Winds Tai Chi Chuan](https://www.fivewinds.co.uk/) was founded on his experience learning from Sifu Cheng Tin Hung in Hong Kong in the early 1970s.

Sometime in the early 2000s Ian encouraged me to start my own class, based in North Berwick. I taught in
the Sports Centre there for about 16 years, until I moved to Oban. In Oban, I briefly taught a classs, but gave up teaching for good when the pandemic hit.

At one point, when teaching in North Berwick, I put together a few notes as a kind of _aide-memoire_ to help me talk to groups of beginners. Here they are. I don't claim any originality - these ideas in one form or another have been expressed many times by others more qualified than I am.
