title: "A Pilgrimage"

excerpt: Imagining a return to the village in Shetland where I grew up.
---

<div class="poem">
I shall find, just past the byre, that once-familiar track
Trudge through the sharn and skirt the ruined concrete tank
Which drank burns and spewed our water, brown with peat
Pass by the rotting posts where stood the cattle gate
Wind through someone else’s yowes to Klondike, thistle-thick
And on.

I shall find in friendly hills the silent Loomi Shun
Where, beguiled by rippling trout-rings, that peerie boy
Once worm-fished in patient unavailing hope
Where faithful rain-goose pair have scraped their nest
And heather-berries still taste sweet and clean
And up.

I shall find the mouldering Tern in memory’s secret noost
Launch her down the beach and hoist the sail
Take her through the narrows, out the voe
Past mackerel grounds and coal-deep Swarbacks Minn
Tack her under the guns, lurching into the cryptic ocean
And beyond.
</div>
