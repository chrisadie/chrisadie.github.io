<div class="poem">
Mr Mack died twice that afternoon
And was efficiently reanimated
For extra pulsation and respiration

He observed how his heart lurched
How his cranium oozed starlight
How his fingers cracked and crackled

He noted how they trundled him down
How the air smelt of worry and drains
How banal it all was

Mr Mack wanted to ask about life
His mouth was full of salt and sawdust
The pain ebbed and ebbed and ebbed and ebbed and

Then everyone said goodbye and turned away
And discreetly readjusted to living
With his fear crouching in their eyes.
